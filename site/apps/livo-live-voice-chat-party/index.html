Livo Live: Voice Chat & Party

By Hangzhou Mocha Network Technology Co., Ltd · Category: Social

The voice-chat app climbing fastest this week — Livo Live: Voice Chat & Party, from Hangzhou Mocha Network Technology, reached 140,000 installs in its first 30 days (all of them in the last month) with a 4.6-star rating and no ads, updated on Nov 18, 2026. It is a live audio community: join voice rooms with up to 36 mics for music lounges, talent shows and late-night chats, host your own room, and send 3D virtual gifts — all without turning on a camera. Pros: no ads at all, a genuinely global community across Asia, the Middle East and Africa, and voice-only rooms that work well on mobile data. Best for Pakistani users who enjoy late-night voice chats, live music rooms and meeting new people without the pressure of video.
