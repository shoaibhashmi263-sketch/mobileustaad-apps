AttaPoll Surveys: Earn Money

By AttaPoll Ltd · Category: Finance

AttaPoll is a UK-based paid survey app that has been on the Play Store since 2015, making it one of the more established legitimate earning apps Pakistani users can actually install. You answer opinion surveys from market-research partners, earn per survey, and can cash out from just $3. Honest expectations matter: Pakistani users see fewer and lower-paying surveys than US or UK users, and with PayPal having no official Pakistan presence, cashing out realistically means gift cards or third-party wallets. Pros: a genuinely long-running legitimate company rather than a fly-by-night scheme, real cash payouts instead of points games, and a low minimum that gets money in hand fast. Cons: survey availability and pay are region-dependent and lower for Pakistan, and payout options are limited without PayPal. Best for Pakistani students and anyone with spare phone time wanting small dollar-denominated pocket money — chai money, not a salary.

  • Paid opinion surveys matched to your profile, each showing a star rating so you pick surveys most likely to complete
  • Payouts from about $0.20 for one-minute surveys up to ~$10 for longer ones
  • Low $3 cash-out threshold — withdraw via PayPal, gift cards or Revolut
  • Extra earning tracks: paid mobile games, app-discovery tasks and a referral program paying ~10% of referrals' survey earnings
  • Profile-based matching that reduces the screen-out rejections common on other survey apps
  • Long-running legitimate operator on the Play Store since 2015 with 10M+ installs
