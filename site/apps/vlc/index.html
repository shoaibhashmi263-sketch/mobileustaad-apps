VLC for Android

By Videolabs · Category: Video & Creativity

VLC for Android brings the famous open-source desktop player to phones, playing virtually every audio and video format with zero ads and no tracking. In Pakistan it is a solid pick for playing downloaded films and songs without sign-ups or subscriptions. Pros: completely free with no ads or in-app purchases, plays every format including network streams, privacy-friendly with no data collection. Cons: interface looks dated next to polished rivals, audio library is basic compared to dedicated music apps. Best for privacy-conscious users who want a no-nonsense, ad-free player.

  • Plays all video and audio formats
  • Network streams and disk shares
  • Equalizer and audio filters
  • Subtitle and multi-track support
  • Media library and folder browsing
  • Picture-in-picture playback
