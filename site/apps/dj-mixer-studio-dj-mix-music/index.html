DJ Mixer Studio & DJ Mix Music

By FunGear inc · Category: Music

Turn your phone into a pocket DJ studio — DJ Mixer Studio & DJ Mix Music holds a 4.2-star rating from 155 reviews on Google Play and passed about 140,000 installs, ranking among the top new free music apps in the US and India. You get two virtual turntables with a crossfader, eight hot cues per deck, loops, BPM sync and tempo controls, plus Beat FX (Echo, Reverb, Flanger), a 3-band equalizer, drum pads and 15+ sample packs — and you can record a mix and save it as MP3. Pros: a genuinely complete DJ toolkit for a free app, the controls are simple enough for a first-timer, and recorded mixes export as standard MP3 files. Cons: it mixes tracks from your own library — it does not stream songs itself — some effects and packs are likely paid extras, and heavy mixing drains battery fast. Best for Pakistani music lovers who want to practise DJing for a mehndi, dholki or college event before ever touching real decks.

  • Dual virtual turntables with a real crossfader for smooth transitions
  • 8 hot cues on each deck plus loops from 1/4 to 4 beats
  • BPM sync, tempo controls and Beat Jump to keep tracks in time
  • Beat FX including Echo, Reverb and Flanger, plus a 3-band equalizer
  • Drum pads and 15+ sample packs for remixes and live beats
  • Record your mixes and export them as MP3 to play again anywhere
