Google Chrome

By Google LLC · Category: Utilities

Chrome is the browser most Pakistanis already use daily, pre-installed on nearly every Android handset and tightly synced with their Google account. Tabs, passwords, and bookmarks follow you from phone to laptop, and it handles Urdu-language sites like Jang, Express, and Dunya News without font issues. Pros: seamless sync across all devices, built-in password manager and autofill, strong Safe Browsing protection against scam sites. Cons: tends to use a lot of RAM and battery on budget phones, and Google's ad and tracking practices make privacy-conscious users uneasy. Best for everyday users who want one browser that works the same everywhere.

  • Sync tabs, bookmarks, and passwords across devices
  • Built-in password manager
  • Safe Browsing malware protection
  • Tab groups and voice search
  • Offline reading list
  • Data-saving Lite browsing mode
