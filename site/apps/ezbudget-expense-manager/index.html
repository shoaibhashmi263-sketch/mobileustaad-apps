EzBudget: Expense Manager

By VIGLAFICO JOINT STOCK COMPANY · Category: Finance

The #1 Top New Free Tools app in India — EzBudget: Expense Manager launched in October 2026, passed 65,000 installs in about three weeks and was updated on Nov 6, 2026, though it has no user ratings yet. It is a deliberately simple pocket accountant: log each expense in a few taps, set a monthly budget for groceries, dining or utilities, and get an alert before you overshoot it, with clean graphs showing where the money actually went and reminders for recurring bills. Pros: genuinely free, a clean interface with no learning curve, budget alerts that work, and a lock to keep your spending private. Cons: no ratings yet so quality is unproven, it contains ads, and you enter every expense by hand — no automatic bank or wallet sync is advertised. Best for Pakistani students and families who want to track monthly kharcha — JazzCash and Easypaisa spending included — without a complicated finance app.

  • Quick expense tracking: add daily transactions in a few taps
  • Flexible budget planner with smart alerts as you near your limits
  • Clear visual reports and graphs of where your money goes
  • Manage cash, bank accounts and cards in one dashboard
  • Recurring bills and reminders so no payment date is missed
  • PIN, fingerprint or Face ID lock keeps your money data private
