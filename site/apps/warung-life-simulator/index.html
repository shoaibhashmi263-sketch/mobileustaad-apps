Warung Life Simulator

By NeoPaw Studio · Category: Gaming

The fastest-rising shop sim of the month — Warung Life Simulator, from NeoPaw Studio, launched on Nov 16, 2026 and already has 340,000 installs, hitting No. 1 in Top Grossing Simulation in Indonesia, with version 1.2 out on Nov 21, 2026. You play Pak Misran, a village shopkeeper in Cianjur working to pay for his son Agus's university fees: order stock through your in-game phone, serve customers before their patience runs out, set prices, and grow the little warung across 10 levels into a proper kiosk, while village life rolls on around you with rain, power cuts, Friday prayers and Ramadan. Indonesian press reported a 4.9-star Play rating from about 3,900 reviews on Nov 23. Pros: a warm, story-driven shop sim with real heart, a living village to explore between shifts, and support for 32-bit phones like the Infinix Smart 9. Cons: the 274 MB download is heavy, the in-game text is Indonesian so non-speakers will miss much of the story, and it is still too new for a settled Play rating. Best for Pakistani players who loved cosy shop games like Fresh Today and want a deeper village story with theirs.

  • Run Pak Misran's village warung: order stock by phone, serve customers and set your own prices
  • Grow through 10 Warung Levels with upgrades like display cases, fans, a radio, QRIS payments, CCTV and WiFi
  • Explore a living village: rice fields, mosque, school and neighbours with their own stories
  • Day and night cycle with rain, power cuts, Friday prayers and Ramadan and Eid seasons
  • In-game phone with chat, online shopping, a cash book, wallet and village map
  • Daily reward calendar and a 1-minute sales duel mode against rival shops
