Apple Music

By Apple Inc. · Category: Music

Apple Music has finally arrived in Pakistan — 11 years after its global debut — and it is immediately a serious rival to Spotify and YouTube Music for local listeners. The headline draws are lossless audio and Spatial Audio at a mainstream price, plus a deep catalogue that includes plenty of Pakistani music alongside global hits. Pros: official availability with local payment options, superior audio quality, and lyrics with translations. Cons: the interface can feel dense if you are switching from YouTube Music, and the free trial is short before the subscription kicks in. Best for listeners who care about sound quality and want a properly licensed, ad-free music service.

  • 100M+ songs, now officially available in Pakistan since October 2026
  • Lossless 24-bit/192 kHz audio plus Spatial Audio for supported phones
  • Offline downloads so commutes with patchy data stay musical
  • Live lyrics with translations for local and international tracks
  • Android Auto and Chromecast support for cars and TVs
  • Concert and tour-date discovery plus collaborative playlists with SharePlay
