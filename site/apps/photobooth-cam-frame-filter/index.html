PhotoBooth Cam: Frame & Filter

By Nova Play Mobile · Category: Photography

PhotoBooth Cam is a fast-growing free camera app (launched August 2026, already charting in Top New Free photography across multiple countries) that turns any selfie or group photo into a framed photobooth-style shot. There is no account signup — open, frame, add stickers, save and share straight to WhatsApp status or TikTok. For Pakistani users who post event and group photos from weddings, Eid gatherings and hangouts, it is a fun, free alternative to Snapchat's filter experience for that category. Pros: completely free with a high 4.7-star rating, genuinely simple no-account workflow, and quick sharing to WhatsApp. Cons: very new so long-term support is unproven, and photobooth framing is niche — limited appeal beyond selfies, parties and events. Best for young Pakistani users who post selfies and group photos to TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp status.

  • Instant photobooth-style capture with live on-screen frame preview
  • 100+ pre-designed frames, updated regularly
  • Sticker packs: emojis, doodles and decorative elements
  • Built-in filters plus basic editing tools for brightness, colour and sizing
  • Import existing gallery photos to frame them retroactively
  • One-tap save and direct share to WhatsApp and social platforms
