Zapya - File Transfer, Share

By Dewmobile USA, Inc. · Category: Utilities

The offline file-sharing app that refuses to die in Pakistan — because it solves a very Pakistani problem: moving huge videos, movies and games between phones without burning mobile data. Zapya sends files of any size over its own direct connection, no Wi-Fi or data needed, and works across Android, iPhone and PC. The group-share and QR-code modes make it the go-to in hostels, shops and family gatherings.

  • Share files of any size and format — online or fully offline
  • No Wi-Fi or mobile data needed for nearby transfers
  • Four connect modes: group, QR code, shake, radar
  • Cross-platform: Android, iOS, Windows and Mac
  • Bulk transfer and whole-folder sharing in one tap
  • Phone replication — move everything to a new device at once
