Moni: Daily Budget Planner

By VINAINCON CONSULTANT COMPANY · Category: Finance

A simple money manager for everyday spending — Moni: Daily Budget Planner launched in October 2026, passed 72,000 installs in three weeks and was updated on Nov 8, 2026 to version 1.0.2, ranking among the top new free apps in India, though it has no user ratings yet. It tracks daily income and expenses by category, lets you set budgets for groceries, bills and entertainment with reminders when you are close to the limit, manages multiple wallets in one place, and reminds you about recurring bills and subscriptions. Pros: clean and simple to use, it is light at about 24MB, and the multi-wallet view plus bill reminders cover the basics well. Cons: no ratings yet so quality is unproven, it contains ads, and as a new app its long-term reliability is still to be proven. Best for Pakistani users who want to track daily kharcha in rupees, plan a monthly household budget and stop overspending before the month ends.

  • Log income and daily expenses in seconds, sorted by category
  • Set custom budgets for groceries, bills, entertainment and more
  • Clear charts and graphs show where your money goes each month
  • Manage multiple wallets — cash, bank accounts, cards and savings
  • Recurring bills and subscription reminders so no due date is missed
  • PIN, fingerprint or face lock keeps your financial data private
