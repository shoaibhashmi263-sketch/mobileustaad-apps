Doku: Binge. Create. Repeat

By dolu play · Category: Entertainment

A short-drama app with a twist — Doku: Binge. Create. Repeat launched in August 2026, passed 100,000 installs with a 4.4-star rating from 279 reviews, and was updated on Nov 6, 2026. Beyond the usual bite-sized romance, thriller and fantasy episodes, Doku lets viewers vote on story ideas and chat with an AI agent to turn their own idea into a series the community can watch. Reviewers like that every episode is genuinely free, with no coins to unlock the next one. Pros: all episodes free, the co-create idea is genuinely different, and it is light on ads for this category. Cons: it comes from a brand-new developer with a small library next to DramaBox and Dramify, AI-written stories can be uneven, and it needs Android 12.1 or newer. Best for Pakistani drama fans who have finished the big short-drama apps and want a fresh, free one where their own story idea could end up on screen.

  • Bite-sized short dramas: romance, thriller, fantasy, campus and more
  • Curated For You feed for quick, immersive binge sessions
  • Vote on story ideas — top-voted ideas get produced into real series
  • Chat with Doku, an AI agent, to turn your own idea into a story
  • All episodes free to watch, with no coins or per-episode payments
  • New original series added regularly
