Ayahfy: Quran & Pray Times

By BitTech Global · Category: Lifestyle

A fast-growing all-in-one Islamic app — Ayahfy: Quran & Pray Times launched in October 2026, passed 250,000 installs and was updated on Nov 9, 2026, though it has no user ratings yet. It puts the full Quran (all 114 Surahs in Arabic with translations), recitation audio, location-based prayer times with Adhan alerts, a Qibla finder, bookmarks, daily duas, a digital Tasbih counter and the Hijri calendar into one free app. Pros: everything in one place instead of three separate apps, prayer times and Qibla work by your location anywhere in Pakistan, and it is free. Cons: no ratings yet so quality is unproven, it contains ads, and it comes from a brand-new developer. Best for anyone in Pakistan who wants Quran reading, prayer reminders and Qibla direction in a single app.

  • Read all 114 Surahs in clear Arabic with translations
  • Listen to Quran recitations and repeat verses for memorisation
  • Prayer times for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha by your location
  • Adhan alerts and prayer reminders
  • Qibla finder to locate the direction of Makkah
  • Bookmarks, daily duas, digital Tasbih counter and Hijri calendar
