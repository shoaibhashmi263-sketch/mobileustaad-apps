Draw Music Maker: Doodle Pad

By BigSoft inc. · Category: Music

The #1 Top New Free Music & Audio app in the US and Italy — Draw Music Maker: Doodle Pad launched on Oct 15, 2026, passed 310,000 installs with a 4.3-star rating from 3,500+ reviews, and was updated on Nov 5, 2026. You draw a line on a 32 x 20 grid and it becomes a melody: height sets the pitch, position sets the timing, and ready-made templates, colour-coded instruments and scales (Pentatonic, Major, Minor) mean nothing ever sounds off-key — no music theory needed. It works fully offline and exports your tune as a WAV file or MIDI. For Pakistani users, that means a free way to sketch a ringtone, background music for a reel, or a first melody with the kids — all without internet. Pros: genuinely fun and free, works offline, and exports real audio files. Cons: it is a sketchpad, not a full music studio, so there is no multi-track editing, and as a brand-new app its long-term support is unproven. Best for anyone in Pakistan who has always wanted to make a tune but never learned an instrument.
