Careem

By Careem · Category: Travel & Transport

Careem is the region's leading everything app, covering rides, food delivery, groceries, home services, and even money transfers in one place. In Pakistan it competes head-on with inDrive and Bykea on rides, while its food and grocery delivery options add everyday convenience. Pros: wide range of ride options, food and grocery delivery in one app, reliable real-time driver tracking. Cons: surge pricing during peak hours, food delivery coverage varies by city. Best for city commuters who also want food and grocery delivery in a single app.
