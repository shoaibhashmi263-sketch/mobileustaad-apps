sastaticket.pk: Flights, Bus

By Sastaticket.pk · Category: Travel & Transport

Pakistan's homegrown travel booking app — sastaticket.pk compares fares across 100+ airlines and books domestic flights on PIA, Airblue, AirSial, SereneAir and Fly Jinnah, plus intercity bus seats on Daewoo, Faisal Movers and other operators, with Umrah and holiday packages in the same app. It highlights the no-withholding-tax, no-hidden-charges angle that matters when booking international tickets in rupees. Pros: one app for flights, buses and Umrah packages, fare comparison across airlines, and transparent pricing with no hidden international charges. Cons: prices move daily so a fare seen today may change tomorrow, and support quality varies during peak travel season. Best for Pakistani travellers booking family trips, Umrah or a Lahore-to-Karachi bus seat without visiting an agent.

  • Flight fare comparison across 100+ airlines, domestic and international
  • Bus tickets with seat selection on major Pakistani operators
  • Umrah packages with flights, hotels and transport bundled
  • Holiday packages to Dubai, Thailand, Malaysia, Turkey and more
  • Sort by cheapest or fastest with layover and baggage details
  • No withholding tax and no hidden international charges
