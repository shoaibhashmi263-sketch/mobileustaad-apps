Video Downloader, Save Videos

By OJ apps · Category: Utilities

Video Downloader, Save Videos is a brand-new entry in Pakistan's favourite utility category (launched September 2026, already ~1.3M installs with a 5.0 rating), combining a private browser, a downloader and an offline player in one lightweight app. Its built-in browser detects videos on any web page for one-tap saving, which is ideal on limited mobile data plans. The honest caveat for every Play Store downloader: it cannot download YouTube videos (Play policy), so the main use is Stories, Reels and status videos from supported sites. Pros: fast-growing and well-reviewed, one app for browsing, downloading and offline playback, and a copyright disclaimer signalling a Play-policy-compliant operator rather than a pirate. Cons: no YouTube downloads, the developer has no established track record, and it is ad-supported. Best for Pakistani users who want to save Reels and status videos for offline viewing — a legitimate Play Store alternative to sketchy sideloaded downloaders like VidMate and Snaptube.

  • Built-in private browser that auto-detects videos on web pages — one-click download without pasting links
  • MP4, HD and 4K quality selection per download
  • Background download manager with multiple simultaneous downloads
  • Built-in offline video player so saved videos play inside the app
  • Listing claims no watermarks and no browsing trace
  • Explicit copyright disclaimer prohibiting downloads of copyrighted material without permission
