Mindrift

By Ai Toloka Inc · Category: Finance

Mindrift is the companion app for the Mindrift platform, run by AI-data company Toloka, where freelance AI Tutors earn dollars by training AI models — rating responses, writing prompts and fixing reasoning errors, all from a phone. It is one of the few legitimate, no-joining-fee ways for Pakistanis to earn foreign-currency income, with payouts in USD via Payoneer. This is skilled project-based work, not a salary — strong English and subject knowledge are needed to pass qualification tests — but skill-based AI tasks pay noticeably better than typical microtask apps.

  • Task alerts for new AI training projects
  • Track completed, in-review and pending-payment tasks
  • In-app balance with withdrawal to Payoneer or Tipalti
  • Direct chat with project leads
  • Qualification exams and project onboarding
  • No joining fee — free to sign up and get assessed
