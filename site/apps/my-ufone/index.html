UPTCL (My Ufone)

By Pak Telecom Mobile Limited · Category: Utilities

Ufone's self-care app, rebranded as UPTCL, is now the unified app for both Ufone 4G and PTCL services, letting users manage mobile and broadband accounts under one login. It offers the popular Spin the Wheel daily game with up to 3GB of free data, plus instant bill payments, recharges, and super-card purchases. Pros: manage Ufone and PTCL in one app, daily spin-the-wheel free data, instant recharges and bill payments. Cons: the recent rebrand merged two app experiences and feels transitional, some users report login issues after the merge. Best for Ufone and PTCL customers wanting one app for both services.

  • Ufone and PTCL account management
  • Balance recharge and bill payment
  • Super Card and bundle purchases
  • Spin the Wheel daily rewards
  • Usage and call detail tracking
  • Biometric login
