UBL Digital

By United Bank Limited · Category: Finance

UBL Digital is United Bank's polished mobile banking app, notable for letting new users open a Smart Account with just a CNIC and biometric verification, with no branch visit needed. It supports transfers via CNIC, mobile number, or QR, plus scheduled bill payments and zakat donations to well-known charities. Pros: instant digital account opening, biometric security with card lock controls, scheduled automatic bill payments. Cons: the app is heavy at over 150MB, some users report login and OTP delays. Best for UBL customers and anyone wanting a fully branchless bank account.

  • Instant digital account opening
  • CNIC, mobile, and QR transfers
  • Scheduled bill payments
  • Zakat donations
  • Card lock/unlock and limit controls
  • Nearby ATM and branch locator
