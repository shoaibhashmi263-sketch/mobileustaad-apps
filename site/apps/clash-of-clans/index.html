Clash of Clans

By Supercell · Category: Gaming

Clash of Clans is the long-running strategy war game where you build a village, train troops, and battle with your clan against players worldwide. It has a large Pakistani player base with many active local clans, so finding teammates in your timezone is easy. Pros: deep strategic gameplay with constant updates, clan wars that keep it social, and it runs well on older devices. Cons: upgrades take a very long time at higher levels, in-app purchases strongly speed up progress. Best for strategy lovers and anyone wanting a long-term social gaming habit.

  • Build and upgrade your village
  • Real-time clan wars
  • Legend League competitive battles
  • Builder Base second village
  • Single-player campaign mode
  • Friendly challenges and events
