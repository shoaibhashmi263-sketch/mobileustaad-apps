Best Apps to Learn English in Pakistan

From spoken English to CSS prep — the apps that actually work, and how to use them.

  1. 1. Duolingo — start here. Ten minutes daily of game-like lessons builds real vocabulary; enable the Urdu-to-English course.
  2. 2. Khan Academy — for academic English and comprehension; free lessons that help with exams too.
  3. 3. BBC Learning English (website + app) — short daily videos on real-world English, free forever.
  4. 4. Practice speaking: record yourself daily and compare — apps teach words, only speaking builds fluency.
  5. 5. Watch English content with English subtitles, not Urdu — dramas and documentaries both work.
  6. 6. Read one Dawn editorial a week and note five new words; apps alone won't build reading stamina.
  7. 7. Consistency beats intensity: 15 focused minutes daily outperforms a 3-hour weekend cram.
