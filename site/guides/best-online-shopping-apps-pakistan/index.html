Best Online Shopping Apps in Pakistan 2026

From Daraz to OLX and Markaz: the best online shopping apps for Pakistani buyers — and how to avoid fake sellers.

  1. 1. Daraz — Pakistan's biggest online marketplace: millions of products from local sellers and brands, with Daraz Mall for verified stores, regular sales and cash on delivery across the country. Our review: /apps/daraz/.
  2. 2. OLX Pakistan — the go-to place to buy and sell used items locally: phones, bikes, cars, furniture and more, listed by people in your own city so you can inspect before paying. Our review: /apps/olx-pakistan/.
  3. 3. AliExpress — order directly from sellers in China, often at the lowest price for gadgets, accessories and phone parts, with buyer protection if the parcel never arrives. Our review: /apps/aliexpress/.
  4. 4. Temu — budget shopping with very low prices and frequent coupons, useful for accessories, home goods and small gadgets if you can wait for international delivery. Our review: /apps/temu/.
  5. 5. Markaz: Pakistan Shopping App — a reselling app built for Pakistan: pick products, share them on WhatsApp and earn a margin on each order while Markaz handles stock, packing and delivery. Our review: /apps/markaz/.
  6. 6. DealSeek: Coupons & Discounts — finds coupons and discount codes for Pakistani stores so you pay less on the same order. Our review: /apps/dealseek-coupons-discounts/.
  7. 7. PakWheels: Buy & Sell Cars — Pakistan's largest car marketplace, for buying or selling a used car or bike with verified listings and price checks. Our review: /apps/pakwheels/.
  8. 8. Zameen.com — Pakistan's biggest property portal, for buying, selling or renting houses, plots and apartments with verified listings. Our review: /apps/zameen/.
  9. 9. Prefer cash on delivery and check the seller's ratings before ordering — never pay in advance to a stranger on WhatsApp or JazzCash for a deal that looks too good to be true.
  10. 10. Compare the same product on Daraz, OLX and AliExpress before buying — the price gap is often thousands of rupees, and DealSeek can cut it further with a coupon.
