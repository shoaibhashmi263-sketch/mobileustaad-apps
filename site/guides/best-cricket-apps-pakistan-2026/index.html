Best Cricket Apps in Pakistan 2026

Live scores, PSL streams and deep stats — the best legal cricket apps for Pakistani fans, plus the clones to avoid.

  1. 1. Cricbuzz — the fastest live scores. Ball-by-ball commentary, squads, toss updates and points tables for PSL, Pakistan internationals, IPL and every major tournament; match alerts arrive in seconds, and the score page loads fast even on weak mobile data.
  2. 2. ESPNcricinfo (Cricinfo app) — the deepest stats desk. Player profiles, records, head-to-heads and proper match reports for fans who ask 'what was his average in Asia since 2021?'; the best pre-match reading and post-match analysis.
  3. 3. CREX — the cleanest scorecard. Quick scorecards without too much reading, covering international matches, leagues and domestic games; the app Pakistan's sports charts rank at the very top.
  4. 4. myco — the Pakistani pick for live cricket. PSL and major ICC matches streamed free with ads, light on data with adjustable stream quality — a favourite when the match is on and the Wi-Fi is not. Our review: /apps/myco/.
  5. 5. tapmad — live sports streaming. PSL, internationals and other live sports in one app, with free and paid tiers depending on the match. Our review: /apps/tapmad-live-sports/.
  6. 6. ARY ZAP — live TV, sports and dramas. A Sports and other ARY channels live on your phone, so one app covers cricket nights and drama evenings alike. Our review: /apps/ary-zap/.
  7. 7. Skip the 'Live Cricket TV HD' clones. Dozens of apps with that name top Pakistan's sports charts, but they stream matches without rights and are packed with aggressive ads — several get removed from the Play Store and some carry malware. Stick to the official apps above.
  8. 8. Tip: scores cost almost no data, video costs a lot. A full T20 on mobile data can eat a weekly bundle, so follow the score on Cricbuzz or CREX and switch to video only for the last overs — or catch the match on Wi-Fi and use the score app for alerts.
