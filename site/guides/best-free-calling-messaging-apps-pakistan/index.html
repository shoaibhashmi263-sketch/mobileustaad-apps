Best Free Calling & Messaging Apps in Pakistan 2026

Free calls, chats and video for Pakistan — WhatsApp, IMO, Telegram and more, with the ones that work best on weak data and budget phones.

  1. 1. WhatsApp — Pakistan's default. Free voice and video calls, chats, Channels and Communities, and it works on the weakest mobile data. Our review: /apps/whatsapp/.
  2. 2. IMO — the overseas-call favourite. Popular with Pakistani families for free international video calls, with simple one-tap calling even on old phones. Our review: /apps/imo/.
  3. 3. Telegram — the big-group messenger. Huge groups and channels, cloud chats that sync across devices, and voice chats for communities. Our review: /apps/telegram/.
  4. 4. Signal — the private one. End-to-end encrypted calls and chats by default, no ads and no trackers, for users who want privacy first. Our review: /apps/signal/.
  5. 5. Messenger — the Facebook link. Free calls and chats with Facebook friends, plus video rooms for group calls. Our review: /apps/messenger/.
  6. 6. Discord — the gamers' hangout. Free voice channels, screen sharing and community servers, a favourite for gaming squads. Our review: /apps/discord/.
  7. 7. WhatsApp Business — for shopkeepers. Free business profile, catalogues and quick replies so small sellers can talk to customers properly. Our review: /apps/whatsapp-business/.
  8. 8. Bitchat — the offline experiment. Bluetooth mesh messaging that works without internet for nearby chats, useful when the network is down. Our review: /apps/bitchat/.
  9. 9. Save data on calls: a WhatsApp voice call uses roughly 0.5 MB per minute, video calls much more — switch to voice on weak signals and turn on 'Use less data for calls' in WhatsApp settings.
